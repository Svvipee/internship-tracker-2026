## About the role
Lydian is a Boston-area sustainable aviation fuel startup (CO2-to-fuel catalyst reactors). The co-op designs, builds, and tests mechatronic equipment that automates catalyst loading in their pilot fuel-synthesis reactor — starting with automated indexing, injection, and inspection tooling. Work includes translating process needs into equipment requirements/test plans, SolidWorks modeling/assemblies/drawings, selecting mechanical/electrical components (actuators, sensors, motors), building and iterating on prototypes and fixtures, and testing/troubleshooting.
