- Factory layout & workstation design using 2D/3D CAD (ergonomic workbenches, assembly tools/fixtures)
- New-product support: BOM review, prototype-build assembly testing
- Writing/documenting work instructions and shop-floor tooling
- Supporting automation & R&D projects (prototyping/testing new manufacturing tools and robotic automation cells)
- Hands-on troubleshooting of assembly issues alongside Production and Quality teams
